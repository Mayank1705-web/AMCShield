PROJECT MISSION

Why AMCShield Exists

RESEARCH

AMCShield studies Automatic Modulation Classification from a robustness perspective: classification accuracy alone is not enough when a model can be exposed to adversarially modified signals.

The platform brings dataset inspection, model training, adversarial attack evaluation, visualization, and report generation into one reproducible workflow.

RESEARCH PIPELINE

From Signal to Evidence

8 STAGES
01Dataset

Inspect signals, classes, SNR distribution, and splits.

02Preparation

Prepare tensors and reproducible data loaders.

03Baseline

Establish standard classifier performance.

04Robust Model

Train the robustness-oriented classifier.

05Attacks

Evaluate FGSM, PGD, MIM, C&W and black-box threats.

06Evaluation

Measure accuracy, ASR, perturbation and gaps.

07Visualization

Expose SNR and attack behavior through charts.

08Reports

Package validated results into reproducible reports.
